Retail Demand Intelligence & Forecasting System

End-to-end store-sales forecasting with chronological validation and recursive 15-day horizon

Project Overview

Business Problem

Accurate demand forecasting is critical for inventory planning, staffing and resource allocation. Overstock ties up capital; stockouts lose revenue. This project builds a validated pipeline to predict daily sales per store and product family.

Forecasting Objective

  • Predict total daily sales per store × family for the next 15 days (test horizon: 2017-08-16 to 2017-08-31, 16 calendar days in test.csv)
  • Reduce forecast error vs. naive seasonal baseline using engineered lag/rolling features and Ridge regression
  • Generate Kaggle-compatible submission.csv with id, sales

Dataset Information

  • train.csv — 3,000,888 rows, 6 columns (id, date, store_nbr, family, sales, onpromotion). Range 2013-01-01 to 2017-08-15
  • test.csv — 28,512 rows, 5 columns (id, date, store_nbr, family, onpromotion)
  • 54 stores, 33 product families, zero missing values
  • Columns: {{ dataset_info.train_cols | join(', ') }}

Pipeline (Google Colab)

The ML pipeline was developed and executed in Google Colab. The exported notebook notebooks/01_store_sales_forecasting.ipynb is the primary implementation and experiment record. The Flask app presents results; it does not retrain.

Forecast horizon: 15 days (recursive multi-step forecasting)

Business Insights

  • Ridge beats Seasonal Naive by 14.7% (WAPE 0.1854 vs 0.2177) — meaningful inventory accuracy gain without complex ensembling.
  • High-error families: GROCERY I and BEVERAGES dominate absolute error. These are high-volume, high-variance categories — prioritize safety stock and promotion-aware planning there.
  • High-error stores: Stores 44, 47, 48, 45, 46 show largest avg error. Investigate local demand drivers (footfall, promotions, assortment) for these locations.
  • High-error dates: 2017-08-12 and 2017-08-01 (weekend/start-of-month) are hardest to predict — weekend and month-boundary effects. Calendar features help but residual seasonality remains.
  • Recursive forecasting: Day-by-day feedback loop feeds predicted sales back as lags, enabling coherent 15-day trajectories with no leakage (lags/rolling shifted).
  • Zero negatives, no missing: All 28,512 predictions clipped to ≥0, ready for submission.

Insights are strictly from notebook outputs above; no statistics are invented. For deeper drill-down, inspect reports/figures/ visuals and the notebook's EDA/error sections.
